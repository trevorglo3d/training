The Automotive Industry Lifecycle & Dealership Software Stack

Chapter 1-1: The Automotive Industry - Key Concepts

Key Players & Terminology

  • OEMOriginal Equipment Manufacturer: The primary manufacturing company that builds vehicles (e.g., Toyota, GM, Ford). (Original Equipment Manufacturer): The company that manufactures the car.
  • Certified / Franchise Dealers: Authorized by OEMs to sell brand-new cars; split into New Car and Used Car departments.
  • Independent Car Dealers: Not affiliated with any specific brand; sell strictly used cars acquired through auto auctions or customer trade-ins.
  • BHPHBuy Here Pay Here: Independent dealers specializing in subprime financing without external third-party lenders. (Buy Here Pay Here): Specializes in buyers with bad credit, offering lower-quality cars and handling financing/leasing in-house.

The 5 Stages of the Car Life Cycle

  1. Manufacturing: The OEM produces the car.
  2. Initial Sale: The car is sent to a Certified/Franchise Dealer.
  3. Consumer Acquisition: Consumers acquire via Lease (finance company owns vehicle), Finance (ownership upon payoff), or Full Cash Payoff.
  4. End of Lease / Ownership: Consumer buyout, dealer repurchase, or transfer to wholesale Dealer Auctions where independent dealers acquire units.
  5. Trade-in / Resale: Resold via Independent lots or Certified Used departments.

Category 1: Digital Merchandising

16 Modules

Basic System Setup & Digital Merchandising Overview

Two Platforms: Web App (glow3d.net) for setup/account management and Mobile App exclusively for vehicle media capture.

  • Display Profile (My Account): Crucial customer-facing display data (logo, name, address). Upscale low-res logos using the built-in Image Upscaler.
  • Capture Aspect Ratio: Must be set before capturing any vehicle (e.g. 16:9, 3:2, or 4:3) to match the dealer's website.
  • Default Backlink: Set primary source to Vehicle Detail Page (VDP) and secondary to static dealer homepage to guarantee backlinks from all social shares.
  • Resolution & Archiving: Capture resolution defaults to 2K; archive setting defaults to Auto archive sold cars.

DM Settings & Customer Skill Adaptation

Skill Level Adaptation: "Bicycle customers" (smaller lots) need simple workflows; "Ferrari customers" (dedicated photographers) can use advanced modes.

  • Smart Camera Mode: Recommended default; automatically balances standard and wide-angle lenses for a natural look without distortion.
  • Plate Blurring & Left/Right Drive: License plate blurring defaults OFF (consumes extra resources). Left-hand drive is North American default.
  • Gallery Styles: Classic Gallery (traditional thumbnail strip) vs. Nova Gallery (modern floating UI). Color match themes using the ColorZilla Chrome extension.
  • Promotional Photos: Insert marketing slides every 3 photos; avoid placing them as the very first image.

Guided Photography Standard Operating Procedure

Captures standardized vehicle galleries in under 5 minutes with automated AI processing:

  • Phase 1: Exterior Money Shots — 8 standard angles starting from passenger side at door-handle height with green on-screen guides.
  • Phase 2: Close-Ups & Open Doors — Switch to Ultra-Wide lens for driver door, rear door, and mirror close-ups.
  • Phase 3: Interior Shots — Sit in the back seat with Ultra-Wide lens for dashboard and center console; move to front for steering wheel controls.
  • Phase 4: Save & AI Processing — Wait for upload to hit 100%; AI removes background, generates video tour with voiceover, adds promotional overlays, and syndicates to website and IMS.

Standard vs. AI Advanced Background Removal

Standard Background: Replaces background with one virtual setting. Turntable should be unchecked for gallery photos (maximizes car size) and checked for 360 spin.

AI Advanced Background (2026 Engine): Gen-AI engine with Left/Center/Right multi-perspective backgrounds, auto-relighting to prevent shadow color cast, clean white Search Results Page (SRP) thumbnails, and High-Point vs. Low-Point sports car camera modes.

360 Exterior Spins & Interior Panoramic Photography

Exterior 360 Capture: 16 frames recommended for smooth spins (8 frames for max speed). Use Standard Lens from 5 steps away; voice control enables hands-free shooting in cold weather.

360 Interior Panoramic: Direct connection to Insta360 (default universal password 88888888) and Ricoh Theta. Camera brand face must point to the front of the car; place on a monopod at eye level over the center console.

Multi-Verticals, VIN Decipher, Web Capture & Badges

  • Other Verticals (Trucks, RVs, Boats): Set Default Vertical to "Ask Every Time" in settings. Full background removal is available for cars; trucks/RVs use thumbnail removal or blur background for spins.
  • Advanced VIN Decipher: Powered by Chrome Data (J.D. Power) to pull exact build data, preventing false advertising liability.
  • Web Capture: Generates a browser link sent via SMS/email for remote sellers to take trade-in appraisal photos without downloading the mobile app.
  • Dynamic Badges: Auto-applied promotional overlays ("EV", "Low Mileage", "Special of Month") that auto-remove if vehicle price/specs change.

Prospecting Methodology: The 4 Fundamentals

  1. Build a Structured Pipeline: Structured prospecting drives clean forecasting and predictable closing rates.
  2. Define and Segment Your ICP: Avoid wasting time on unlicensed lots or seasonal dealers; prioritize lots with 50–200 cars.
  3. Map Key Vendors: Research the dealer's website provider (e.g. Dealer Center, CDK) before calling to pinpoint pain points.
  4. Leverage Targeted Outreach: Account-based cold calling with researched inventory metrics consistently outperforms random dials.

Market Segmentation

Tier 1: Independent Lots (0–90 units) Search state DMV active dealer lists. Short 1–3 day sales cycle with direct owner access.
Tier 2: Mid-Market (100–250 units) Target General Managers (GMs) using local dealership references for social proof.
Tier 3: Enterprise (500+ units) Target corporate executives via Apollo Access; 6–12 month sales cycle for multi-rooftops.
